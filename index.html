Your Tree of Learning

Grow a language.
Grow your world.

Start with curiosity. Build roots in sound, script, and meaning. Branch into culture and conversation, and let your own expression blossom.

Taking root

Introduce yourself, ask everyday questions, and begin reading familiar Arabic words. Discover word families and build a practice you can return to.

A first milestone: a supported 1–2-minute introduction.

Branching out

Keep a familiar conversation going, tell a simple story, and write a short message. Explore a song, a cultural story, or a spiritual idea with guidance.

A working target: a 5–7-minute familiar-topic conversation.

Blossoming

Share experiences, opinions, and feelings. Create a story, video, reflection, or song performance in Arabic, and learn with greater independence.

A working target: a 10–15-minute familiar-topic conversation.

Which Arabic will I learn?

Egyptian Arabic is the core of learning and conversation. We also explore relevant Standard Arabic to support your understanding of the language, without making advanced formal Arabic the focus.

Can I join if I already speak some Arabic?

Yes. We welcome complete beginners through advanced learners and organize activities by level. Earlier and more advanced challenges give you ways to practice beyond the weekly class.

How much time should I set aside?

Plan for 4–6 hours a week across lessons, activities, and practice. Motivated learners can build toward more self-directed practice, up to around 20 hours weekly using content and varied application.

What if I cannot attend live?

Weekly class recordings let you revisit the material. Recorded workshops, learning resources, and community challenges also give you ways to practice between live sessions.

How do spirituality and religion fit into the lessons?

We explore spiritual stories, ethical ideas, Sufism, and Abrahamic traditions through their language and cultural context, without religious bias. Álquimia is a language and cultural learning community, not a course in religious doctrine.

How do payment and access work?

Choose monthly or annual, add your details, and complete payment on Stripe. After confirming payment, Hapi sends your community invitation and access information. Your signup alone does not confirm payment.

Are the monthly and annual memberships different?

Both give you the same full access. Monthly is US$25; annual is US$250 for 12 months, saving US$50 compared with 12 monthly payments.
