Ask Álquimia on WhatsApp ↗
Which Arabic will I learn?

Our focus is Egyptian Arabic for daily communication, alongside writing foundations and cultural learning. We explore its connection with Egypt and the wider Arabic-speaking world.

Can I join as a beginner?

Yes. The membership includes foundational lessons, quizzes, and exercises. You can begin with the foundations or use the community activities to practice what you already know.

Are English and Portuguese sessions separate?

Yes. There are separate English and Portuguese live sessions each week, with captions and recordings. Choose your preferred session language when you join.

What if I can’t attend live?

Weekly sessions are recorded, so you can revisit the material during your membership.

Do monthly and annual members get the same access?

Yes. Monthly and annual members receive the same full access. Select USD or BRL above to see the price and annual savings in your preferred payment currency.

How do payment and community access work?

Choose your plan and payment currency, then enter your name, email, country, and preferred session language. We save your signup details privately before opening secure checkout: Stripe for US dollars or InfinitePay for Brazilian reais. Use the same email at checkout so we can match your payment. After payment is confirmed, the Álquimia team manually invites you to NAS.com and shares the private WhatsApp group and weekly meeting details.

Can I arrange private classes?

Private classes are organized separately, directly with Álquimia. Contact the team on WhatsApp to discuss your needs.
