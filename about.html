Meet Hapi

An Egyptian voice.
An open invitation.

I created Álquimia to share the language through the life around it.

My love of Egypt runs through its conversations, music, history, art, and the stories behind its words. Teaching brings those interests together with something just as important: human connection.

In Álquimia, I want learners to experiment, develop their voice, and build a relationship with Arabic that keeps growing.

Talk with me about Álquimia

The alchemy
is in the journey.

Curiosity becomes practice. Practice becomes skill. Skill becomes connection.

Álquimia brings language, history, sound, spirituality, art, and human experience into a living cultural journey.

We invite poetic expression, wit, thoughtful reflection, and an openness to other people. Each learner brings their own interests and experiences to that process.

Egyptian Arabic is our starting point. Egypt’s cultural layers and the wider Arabic-speaking world give the journey depth.

Our mission

Empower 1,000,000 non-Arabic speakers to speak Egyptian Arabic and live the language through culture, creativity, and community.

Our long-term ambition—not a current learner count.

Our community

Welcome learners from complete beginners to advanced levels. Bring people together through live lessons, practical activities, creative challenges, and shared discovery.

Our approach

Make learning immersive, playful, practical, and meaningful. Explore spiritual and religious ideas through language and culture with care and without religious bias.
