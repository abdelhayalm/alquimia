THE IDEA BEHIND THE SCHOOL

What if learning
felt like entering
a culture?

LANGUAGECULTURECONNECTION

Álquimia brings together Hapi’s work in education, his love of Egyptian culture, and his experience moving between languages. Its starting point is a question: how can learning Arabic become something people feel, practise, and use?

Egypt offers a rich place to begin: the rhythm of a greeting, a lyric you want to understand, a story carried through cinema, a letter shaped by hand. Each can become an invitation to look closer and join in.

The idea is to give those moments a home: a community school where thoughtful materials, purposeful play, and real conversation turn curiosity into practical skills.

WHAT GUIDES US

Depth in the content.
Warmth in the experience.

The way we build the school matters as much as the things we teach.

Curiosity.

Follow a question. Notice a connection. Leave room for discovery and a perspective beyond your own.

Depth & care.

Create thoughtfully researched materials and give language its cultural context. Explore Egypt’s ancient heritage and modern diversity with care.

Make space for beginners, different backgrounds, and the courage to try. Grow through listening, participation, and mutual respect.

THE PERSON BEHIND ÁLQUIMIA

An Egyptian voice.
A shared journey.

Hapi is an Egyptian educator based in Brazil, with experience teaching more than 2,000 students worldwide and online. He brings Arabic, English, and Portuguese into a practice rooted in communication and cultural understanding.

His passion for Egypt, language, history, and creativity shapes the school’s materials. He looks for the depth behind a word or a story, then turns it into an approachable learning experience.

You’ll meet him weekly, learning and practising alongside the Álquimia team. Clear teaching, encouragement, and edutainment are central to that shared journey.
